## Built for training. Designed for performance.

**TeamDesk** is a sports training, coaching and athlete performance platform for basketball, handball and other team sports. Its core product direction is to help coaches plan training, organize exercises and playbooks, monitor athletes and interpret performance information.

**TeamDesk is not primarily a club CRM.** Team administration is a supporting capability; the core product is **training, coaching, athlete development and analytics**.

The public PROCESS IN registry lists the TeamDesk domain as owner-reported LIVE. That does not independently verify current implementation, commercial availability or the completion of planned modules.

## Core product direction

| Area | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Training Planner** | Plan sessions, training blocks, drills and coaching objectives |
| **Coaching Workspace** | Structure training workflows and team preparation |
| **Playbook** | Organize sport-specific plays, exercises and tactical material |
| **Athlete Monitoring** | Track athlete development, observations and workload indicators |
| **Performance Analytics** | Turn training and athlete data into useful coaching insights |
| **Multi-sport Design** | Basketball, handball and extensibility to other sports |
| **Team Operations** | Supporting team, roster and club administration |
| **AI-assisted Workflows** | Explore practical intelligence for coaching and analysis |

> These are product scope and roadmap areas, not a verified checklist of released functionality. AI features, integrations, measurements and commercial terms must be confirmed before use.

## Who TeamDesk serves

**Coaches** who plan and review training, **performance staff** who monitor athlete development, **sports clubs and academies** that need consistent coaching workflows, and **athletes** participating in structured training programs.

## Product priorities

1. **Training first** — planning, exercises and session execution are central.
2. **Athlete progress** — the platform should help understand development over time.
3. **Useful analytics** — data should support practical coaching decisions.
4. **Coach-friendly UX** — fast, clear workflows before administrative complexity.
5. **Club administration second** — supporting operations without displacing the core sports purpose.
