## Training first. Performance always.

**[TeamDesk](https://teamdesk.mk)** is a PROCESS IN digital product with a focused mission: Structure sessions, training cycles, drill sequences, intensity and coaching objectives. The product connects its central use case with a recognizable brand experience, practical workflows and a clear path for the people who use it.

It is presented here as a **real product showcase**, not an open-source code repository. The areas below describe the product's functional scope and intended use; individual capabilities, account access and commercial terms should be confirmed through the official product channel.

## How TeamDesk works

| Stage | Practical meaning |
|---|---|
| **01 — Define athletes, sport and coaching objectives** | Establish the starting context and what success should look like. |
| **02 — Plan training cycles and individual sessions** | Translate the objective into a structured product workflow. |
| **03 — Organize drills, exercises and tactical material** | Bring the relevant content, people or activities into focus. |
| **04 — Record observations and athlete workload** | Review information and make the next action understandable. |
| **05 — Review development and performance trends** | Move from exploration toward a concrete outcome. |
| **06 — Refine the next training cycle** | Keep the process useful as needs evolve. |
